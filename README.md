# Credit Card Transaction & Customer Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An end-to-end 4-page Power BI executive report analyzing **$57M in total revenue**, **667K transactions**, **$8M in interest earned**, and customer demographics across card tiers and income groups.

## Report Structure
1. **Credit Card Transaction Report:** Tracks overall revenue ($57M), transaction volume (667K), quarterly trends, card categories, and expenditure segments.
2. **Revenue Growth & Root Cause Analysis:** Interactive **Decomposition Tree** paired with **QoQ (+1.8%)** and **WoW (+28.8%)** time-intelligence KPIs.
3. **Credit Card Customer Report:** Evaluates $588M customer income, Customer Satisfaction Score (3.19 CSS), and demographic splits across age, marital status, and dependents.
4. **Key Insights & Recommendations:** Actionable business strategy addressing customer acquisition ROI and spend drivers.

## Key Business Insights & Recommendations
- **Female Acquisition vs. Revenue Gap:** Despite higher acquisition spend on females ($566K for 5.99K users) than males ($425K for 4.31K users), female revenue lags at $26M compared to $31M from males. *Recommendation:* Reallocate acquisition budget toward targeted cashback perks on Bills and Entertainment.
- **Top Revenue Drivers:** Blue Card holders ($47M), Graduates ($23M), and Businessmen ($18M) drive the highest revenue, with Swipe transactions accounting for $36M.
- **Seasonal Momentum:** Q4 revenue grew by $0.50M to $14.50M (+1.8% QoQ vs. $14M in Q3), fueled by a 28.8% Week-over-Week surge in Week-53.
